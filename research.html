Muhammad Siddique, Dr. Faisal Hayat (UET), Dr. Yasir Saleem (UET)

University of Engineer and Technology Journal, 2025

Role of Experiential Learning in Bridging the Academia-Industry Gap

Muhammad Siddique, et. al.

ACADEMIA International Journal for Social Sciences (AIJSS), 2025

In order to bridge the gap between academics and industry, this study paper delves into the crucial function of experiential learning. It gives some background and then dives into how experiential learning helps students build skills and critical thinking in preparation for the job by focusing on practical applications and hands-on experiences.
